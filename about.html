About LIVIO Residences

Housing should be simpler.

We are building a better way to find homes, connect tenants with property owners, and manage residential properties. LIVIO brings the housing experience into one connected platform.

Who We Are

More than finding a place to live.

LIVIO Residences is a housing and property management platform designed to connect people with homes and simplify the relationship between tenants, landlords and property managers.

We recognise that finding a home can be stressful. Unclear property information, unreliable listings, poor communication and complicated property management can make the experience difficult.

Our goal is to bring these activities together through technology, transparency and organised property services.

We believe that finding and managing a home should be an organised, transparent and reliable experience.

Our Direction

Building around real housing needs.

We want to make the housing journey easier for everyone involved, from the first property search to everyday property management.

Our Mission

To simplify housing through accessible technology, trusted connections and efficient property services.

Our Vision

To build a trusted and connected housing ecosystem that improves how people find homes and how properties are managed.

What We Do

One platform. Multiple housing solutions.

LIVIO is designed to support the different people and activities involved in residential housing.

Find a Home

Explore available properties, compare options, request viewings and make informed housing decisions.

Property Management

Give property owners and managers tools to organise properties, vacancies, tenants and daily operations.

Property Verification

Introduce a review process to check property information and supporting ownership or rental-authority documents before listings are published.

Tenancy Management

Organise tenancy information, rental records, applications, agreements and communication.

Maintenance Coordination

Help tenants report property issues and allow landlords and managers to coordinate repairs.

Relocation Support

Connect people moving homes with relevant relocation and moving service providers.

What Guides Us

The principles behind LIVIO.

The foundation of our platform is not just technology. It is how that technology serves people.

Trust

Encourage transparent interactions and responsible property information.

People First

Design services around the actual needs of tenants, landlords and property managers.

Innovation

Use practical technology to address real challenges in the housing sector.

Transparency

Promote clear information, understandable processes and responsible service delivery.

Who We Serve

Designed for everyone in the housing journey.

Tenants and Home Seekers

People looking for suitable homes, arranging viewings or managing their tenancy.

Landlords

Property owners looking for better ways to list and manage their rental properties.

Property Managers

Professionals responsible for coordinating property operations and tenant relationships.

Be Part of the Journey

A better housing experience starts here.

Whether you are searching for a home or managing property, LIVIO is being built to make the process easier and more organised.
