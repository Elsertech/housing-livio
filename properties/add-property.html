02. Ownership & Management

Tell us in what capacity you are submitting this property.

Property verification is mandatory

LIVIO requires evidence of ownership or valid management authority. Your property will not be published until the verification process is complete.

03. Property Features

Describe the facilities and features available.

04. Rental Units

Add the individual units available within this property. Each unit will have its own rental information and gallery.

One property can contain multiple units

For example, Greenview Apartments may contain bedsitters, studios, one-bedroom and two-bedroom units. Each unit will become a separate rental listing after approval.

Unit registration comes next

After saving your property details, you will be able to register individual units, set monthly rent, specify availability and upload unit-specific photos.

05. Property Photos & Documents

Prepare the supporting materials for LIVIO review.

Media and document uploads

The secure upload centre will allow you to submit property images, ownership documents and management agreements. These files will be stored privately until reviewed.

Secure uploads will be enabled in the next development step.
Before submitting

Submitting a property does not automatically publish it. LIVIO must independently review ownership or authority evidence and verify the property before it becomes visible to tenants.
